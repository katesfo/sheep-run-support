SHEEP RUN!

A cheerful farm escape for iPhone and iPad — outrun Farmer Dave!

Support

How do I play?

Tap anywhere to jump. Hold for a big leap, let go early for a short hop. Don't let Farmer Dave catch you.

There's no sound

Tap the note button on the title screen to unmute, and check your phone's silent switch — the game politely respects it.

Where is my best score kept?

On your device, and only on your device. Deleting the app deletes your best escape too, so the flock recommends bragging about it first.

Found a bug, or have an idea?

Leave a review on the App Store describing it — reviews are read and the game is updated regularly. New obstacles, fixes and silliness arrive through normal App Store updates.

Privacy Policy

Sheep Run! does not collect any data. At all.

There are no accounts, no sign-ins, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and the game never connects to the internet. Your best escape distance is saved only on your own device and never leaves it.

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to share, sell, or lose. If these practices ever change, this page will be updated first.

Run free, little sheep.
